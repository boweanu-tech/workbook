\TypeQuestion{日本語に合うように、（　）内の適切な語句を丸で囲み、完成した正しい英文を書きなさい。}{generalverb_question_fill_choice}
\SetRowSpace{2mm}
\EngWriteQuestion{あなたは英語を勉強しますか。\par\EngSentence{\EngChoice{Do / Are} you study English?}}{Do you study English?}

\EngWriteQuestion{彼らは忙しくありません。\par\EngSentence{They \EngChoice{aren't / don't} busy.}}{They aren't busy.}
\EngWriteQuestion{私たちはテニスをします。\par\EngSentence{We \EngChoice{are / play} tennis.}}{We play tennis.}

\EngWriteQuestion{あなたは猫が好きですか。\par\EngSentence{\EngChoice{Are / Do} you like cats?}}{Do you like cats?}

\TypeQuestion{疑問文に対する答えとして最も適切なものを一つ選び、その記号を丸で囲みなさい。\par 疑問文と選んだ返答を、続けて日本語に訳しなさい。}{generalverb_question_response_choice}
\SetRowSpace{2mm}
\EngResponseChoice{Do you play baseball?}{\mbox{（ア）\EngText{Yes, I am.}}\qquad\mbox{（イ）\EngText{Yes, I do.}}\qquad\mbox{（ウ）\EngText{Yes, you do.}}}{\parbox[t]{\linewidth}{イ\par あなたは野球をしますか。\par はい、します。}}

\TypeQuestion{日本語に合うように、〔　〕内のすべての語句を並べ替えて、正しい英文を作りなさい。}{generalverb_question_reorder}
\SetRowSpace{2mm}
\EngWriteQuestion{あなたはテニスをしますか。\par\EngReorder{you / tennis / do / play}}{Do you play tennis?}
\EngWriteQuestion{彼らはこのコンピューターを使いますか。\par\EngReorder{this computer / they / use / do}}{Do they use this computer?}
\EngWriteQuestion{あなたは生徒ですか。\par\EngReorder{you / a student / are}}{Are you a student?}
\EngWriteQuestion{彼らは忙しいですか。\par\EngReorder{busy / they / are}}{Are they busy?}
\EngWriteQuestion{これはあなたの本ですか。\par\EngReorder{your book / this / is}}{Is this your book?}
\EngWriteQuestion{あなたは音楽が好きですか。\par\EngReorder{music / you / do / like}}{Do you like music?}
\EngWriteQuestion{私たちは友達です。\par\EngReorder{friends / we / are}}{We are friends.}
\EngWriteQuestion{私は犬が好きではありません。\par\EngReorder{dogs / like / don't / i}}{I don't like dogs.}

\TypeQuestion{もとの英文を疑問文に書き換え、指示に従って短く答えなさい。}{generalverb_question_transform}
\SetRowSpace{3mm}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{You play tennis.}\quad〔\EngText{Yes}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Do you play tennis?}{\EngText{Yes, I do.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{They study English every day.}\quad〔\EngText{No}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Do they study English every day?}{\EngText{No, they don't.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{You are busy.}\quad〔\EngText{Yes}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Are you busy?}{\EngText{Yes, I am.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{They are students.}\quad〔\EngText{No}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Are they students?}{\EngText{No, they aren't.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{This is your book.}\quad〔\EngText{Yes}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Is this your book?}{\EngText{Yes, it is.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{You like dogs.}\quad〔\EngText{No}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Do you like dogs?}{\EngText{No, I don't.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{They use this room.}\quad〔\EngText{Yes}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Do they use this room?}{\EngText{Yes, they do.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{He is your friend.}\quad〔\EngText{No}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Is he your friend?}{\EngText{No, he isn't.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{You read books.}\quad〔\EngText{Yes}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Do you read books?}{\EngText{Yes, I do.}}}
\EngWriteQuestion{\EngSentence{We are friends.}\quad〔\EngText{No}で〕}{\EngAnswerPair{Are we friends?}{\EngText{No, we aren't.}}}
